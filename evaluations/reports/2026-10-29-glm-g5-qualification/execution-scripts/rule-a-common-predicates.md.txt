## Common machine-checkable requirements

Apply these predicates to every candidate; a retry cannot waive one:

1. Exactly the frozen three development cases × four arms × three repeats = **36 logical cells**, no replacement of cases or repeats. Freeze profile, wheel/source/prompt/corpus identities before a future run, only after Jason's decision. Every started attempt has a unique identity, terminal status, artifacts, usage and cause; interrupted/unknown/unaccountable attempts fail the schedule. An unstarted planned session is reported as unstarted, never counted as success or added to the failure-rate denominator.
2. **Zero paid calls and zero attempted fallbacks**, verified from retained routing/provider records for every qualification attempt and retry. No overrun fallback, provider/model substitution, budget increase, prompt change or extra correction on retry. Existing bounded correction turns are part of their original session and their tokens, not fresh session retries; only the terminal session status determines its failed-session count.
3. A **failed session** is any started session whose authoritative engine/validator terminal status is not `complete`, including `partial/budget`, caps, timeouts, schema/protocol/citation failures and repetition. HTTP success or proxy nominal spend does not override it. Count each distinct started session once, across discovery, assessment, discussion, corrections' owning sessions and every retry. A failed whole cell is not itself another session failure.
4. **Complete usage ledger across all attempts**, including failed requests, tool turns, constrained/correction turns and cache categories. Reconcile engine usage against uniquely attributed retained provider records. Supplement a missing engine request once, with its identity/cell and source binding; do not silently replace `accounting_complete:false` with true. Unattributable/missing usage fails the schedule. Every original/recovery phase retains the frozen per-attempt shared token ceiling T (currently 200,000), unchanged session grants, turn bounds, output caps and timeout. Report cumulative operation cost separately; no token reset erases failed spend. An overshoot remains a failed session and is fully charged.
5. **No failed-session answer is usable.** For A/C, discard the entire failed cell attempt's answers from acceptance. For B, only successful sessions with exactly matching immutable inputs may be retained; failed answers and all descendants of changed inputs are unusable. Re-run current source/evidence/identity validators and the existing deterministic verdict code. Do not edit/drop/clamp findings, citations, hashes or IDs; no model-issued verdict is authoritative.
6. **36/36 final logical cells must complete with accounted, validated accepted execution**, all 12 clean cells included. All six aggregate defect cells need nonempty valid v2 evidence. All six assessed defect cells need an assessor that actually ran (usage >0), finished successfully and has complete accounting against the accepted candidate set. Retain equivalent phase validity in the focused arms. In addition, explicitly require nonempty valid evidence in **every one of the 24 defect cells**, as requested here; this strengthens the old nonempty check outside the aggregate arm. The historical successful cells satisfy it. Completed but empty/invalid evidence is a gate failure, **not** permission to retry for a better answer.
7. `holdout_used == false`; no holdout content is opened, used for tuning, or included in this analysis. Mechanical evidence validation is not human confirmation of a true positive. Keep automated matches, pending judgments, latency and all failures visible; no semantic quality promotion follows from this gate.

Accounting and execution are separate: a fully charged failed session stays failed. A successful rerun satisfies evidence/assessor predicates only through its own validated output and completed assessor; failed first-attempt evidence never fills a missing cell.
